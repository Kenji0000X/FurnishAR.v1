# Attribution

`SKILL.md` in this directory adapts ideas and checklist wording from two
open-source projects, condensed to a single static checklist and re-pointed
at FurnishAR's own tokens (`public/styles.css`, `BRAND.md`) and scripts
(`scripts/check-contrast.mjs`, `scripts/check-home-sections.mjs`,
`scripts/check-mobile.mjs`) instead of their original external binaries and
generic recipes:

- **taste-skill** — <https://github.com/leonxlnx/taste-skill> — MIT License.
- **impeccable** — <https://impeccable.style> — Apache License 2.0.
  The "Verify" / "Refuse" checklist and the five-dimension scored audit in
  `SKILL.md` are condensed from its `craft-floor.md` and `audit.md` reference
  files.

Neither project's stylistic recipes (their specific color palettes, font
picks, or named "visual archetypes" such as industrial-brutalist or
bento-grid minimalism) were carried over — FurnishAR's own `BRAND.md` already
defines those choices for this product, and adopting a second, conflicting
palette from an unrelated skill would contradict it. What was kept is the
project-agnostic part: the quality-floor checklist, the "these are AI
defaults, not decisions" refusal list, and the audit rubric shape.
